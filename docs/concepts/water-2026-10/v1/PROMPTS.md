# Промпты WATER v1

Способ: встроенный `image_gen`, два самостоятельных вызова, непрозрачные концепт-макеты. Не CLI/API. Исходники сохранены рядом как `a-earthen.png` и `b-village.png`; игровые ресурсы не заменены.

## A

```text
Create a polished LANDSCAPE 1536x1024 art-direction concept for a cozy top-down 2D farming RPG, in a restrained hand-painted storybook style compatible with Stardew Valley and Graveyard Keeper but NOT copying either game's assets. This is a separate concept sheet, not a finished background asset. Option A: NATURAL EARTHEN RIVERBANK. Orthographic view looking straight down with a slight readable front face for river banks, no isometric rotation. A river of muted teal blue water travels strictly horizontally and vertically with one generous 90-degree elbow, not diagonally and not a staircase of tiny steps. Continuous narrow grassy rim, an exposed small warm-earth cut bank with realistic soft contact shadow, pale turquoise shallow water transitioning gently to deeper desaturated teal. Slow subtle surface streaks suggest gentle motion; very few soft highlights, no white noisy foam. Add one simple wooden pedestrian bridge precisely horizontal across a vertical straight river section, with both approaches on solid land. One small standalone bench on land faces down toward the viewer with horizontal seat, never diagonal. A small blue-coated brown-haired farmer standing nearby establishes scale: main river 3-4 character-footprint widths, bank very narrow. Few reeds rooted at bank, a cluster of lily pads on water. Muted moss/sage meadow and simple warm earth path, generous calm areas, clear silhouette and clean soft edges, detailed yet not coarse pixel art. Consistent sunlight upper left. Main picture occupies left three quarters. Right quarter has three neat close-up panels showing a straight bank, an inner concave corner, and an outer corner, all using the exact same style and scale. Clearly show independent reusable bank sprites rather than a single ornate landscape. Small heading 'A — EARTHEN BANK'. No HUD, no minimap, no numbers or UI panels, no photographic water, no white outlines, no giant rocks floating in water, no buildings, no diagonal banks, no 3D perspective.
```

## B

```text
LANDSCAPE 1536x1024 polished concept sheet for a cozy hand-painted top-down farming RPG. This is Option B: SMALL VILLAGE STONE-LINED WATERCOURSE. Calm desaturated teal realistic-looking illustrated water, shallow pale green edges, small narrow old sandstone and timber reinforcement at the bank, moss and grass on dry land. No isometric perspective. All long riverbank edges MUST BE EXACTLY horizontal or vertical relative to the image, with 90-degree bends. Absolutely no diagonal shoreline or trapezoid perspective: draw the river in a flat orthographic PLAN, a right-angle L with vertical segment x=440..620 from the top to y=700, then horizontal segment x=440..1060 y=520..700. Thick bank edge only 12-18px, tiny soft contact shadows suggest a small depth. Water subtle smooth streaks, quiet and clear, not noisy busy sparkles. Narrow stone coping and occasional straight wooden village guardrails along dry paths, leave one safe fishing opening. One wooden pedestrian bridge runs exactly horizontally over the vertical water at y=290, both ends safely on grass/path. Tiny blue-coated brown-haired farmer at the dry approach establishes scale. Benches only face straight down or sideways, never diagonally. Cozy muted moss/sage green ground with sparse flowers and reeds, consistent upper-left sunlight, clean high-resolution painterly sprites rather than coarse pixelated texture. Main illustration on left 70 percent. Right 30 percent has three clearly separated close-up tiles illustrating straight stone bank, concave 90-degree corner, convex 90-degree corner. Small title 'B — VILLAGE BANK'. No game HUD, no labels except title, no ornamental fantasy decor, no floating rocks on water, no photographic water, no giant embankments, no buildings. Prioritize modular reusable narrow river edges and perfectly axis-aligned geometry.
```
